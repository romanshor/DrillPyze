# DrillPyze 1.1.0

**DrillPyze 1.1** adds energy accounting: where the power put into drilling goes, in real time, and how much a hybrid rig could recover by regenerative braking.

- **Try it in your browser:** https://romanshor.github.io/DrillPyze/lite/ (the energy panel is under the drilling log; in demo mode it is the fourth page)
- **Documentation:** https://romanshor.github.io/DrillPyze/ (derivation §11, physics specification revision 4)
- **Changelog:** [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/romanshor/DrillPyze/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)

## What's new

- **Power in:** the Mud Pumps (SPP·Q over a pump efficiency, 90 % by default), the top drive, the drawworks hoisting, and gravity lowering the string.
- **Power out:** Rock Destruction at the bit (from the string and the mud motor), wall friction and damping, Frictional Losses inside the string and in the Annulus, the bit nozzles, the mud motor's losses, and energy stored in the string.
- **Regenerative braking:** the braking of the top drive (negative torque) and of the drawworks (tripping in, and drilling at ROP), and the part a regenerative drive could store (90 % by default). Tripping Well A to 2500 m brakes 255 kWh, 230 kWh of it recoverable.
- **Exact bookkeeping:** accumulated every time step; the balances close to the scheme's numerical dissipation (about 0.6 %). The browser engine matches the Python model to round-off.
- **Simulator:** a Sankey diagram, power over time and summary tiles, as 1, 10 or 60 s means, in kW or hp; energies in kWh or MWh.
- **API:** `Simulation.energy()`, `DrillPyze.energy.flows`, `EnergyParameters` and `examples/tripping_energy.py`.

Existing cases give the same results as in 1.0.0: the accumulators change no state.
